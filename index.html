Research overview

From cellular behavior to tissue level remodeling

My work connects experimental tissue engineering, longitudinal biological imaging, and reproducible quantitative analysis.

01

Functional bone models

Multicellular three dimensional systems that reproduce sequential bone formation, resorption, and reformation.

02

Advanced imaging

Longitudinal two photon microscopy, SHG matrix imaging, fluorescence tracking, and tissue clearing.

03

Quantitative analysis

Imaris based volumetric workflows, MATLAB pipelines, spatial analysis, and repeated measures statistics.

Current focus

PTH regulated bone remodeling

My doctoral research examines how PTH(1 to 34) dose and exposure pattern influence osteoblast and osteoclast interactions across formation, resorption, and reformation phases.
