Research overview

From cellular behavior to tissue level remodeling

My work connects experimental tissue engineering, longitudinal biological imaging, and reproducible quantitative analysis.

01

Functional bone models

Multicellular three dimensional systems that reproduce sequential bone formation, resorption, and reformation.

02

Advanced imaging

Longitudinal two photon microscopy, SHG matrix imaging, fluorescence tracking, and tissue clearing.

03

Quantitative analysis

Imaris based volumetric workflows, MATLAB pipelines, spatial analysis, and repeated measures statistics.

Current focus

PTH regulated bone remodeling

My doctoral research examines how PTH(1 to 34) dose and exposure pattern influence osteoblast and osteoclast interactions across formation, resorption, and reformation phases.

Bone tissue engineering PTH signaling Osteoblasts Osteoclasts Osteocyte biology Biomaterials

Professional profile

Building an independent path in regenerative bone research

My long term goal is to become an independent researcher developing controllable tissue engineering platforms for regenerative bone medicine.

My current work focuses on three dimensional functional bone models, PTH regulated remodeling, osteoblast and osteoclast coupling, second harmonic generation imaging, tissue clearing, and quantitative three dimensional analysis. I am particularly interested in platforms that connect mechanistic studies with biomaterial design and therapeutic regeneration.

Technical profile

Methods and capabilities

Bone biology and tissue engineering

Three dimensional functional bone models, osteospheroid models, remodeling assays, PTH experiments, osteoblast, osteoclast, and osteocyte related workflows.

Imaging and visualization

Two photon microscopy, SHG imaging, light microscopy, electron microscopy, micro CT, Imaris, NIS Elements, ImageJ, and Fiji.

Molecular and wet laboratory

qPCR, single cell PCR, multicellular labeling, staining, DNA recombination, transfection, transduction, and drug administration studies.

Quantitative analysis

MATLAB workflows for image derived measurements, repeated measures analysis, multiple comparisons, data visualization, and working knowledge of Python and R.

Tissue clearing

CUBIC and LUCID protocols for internal cellular organization, matrix visualization, and three dimensional reconstruction.

Research communication

Scientific writing, manuscript preparation, peer review response, visual communication, and reproducible code documentation.
