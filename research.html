Research overview

Controllable systems for studying bone remodeling

My central objective is to develop controllable tissue engineering systems that connect mechanistic bone biology with translational regenerative strategies.

01
Doctoral research

Functional bone remodeling models

I develop and maintain multicellular three dimensional bone models that reproduce sequential tissue formation, resorption, and reformation. These systems allow controlled investigation of osteoblast and osteoclast coupling within a self-generated bone-like extracellular matrix.

Long-term 3D culture Bone remodeling Multicellular systems Matrix regeneration
02
Pharmacologic regulation

PTH exposure and remodeling dynamics

I investigate how intermittent and continuous PTH(1-34) exposure patterns regulate tissue-level remodeling. The work examines matrix loss and recovery, cellular distribution, migration, spatial coupling, and molecular readouts associated with bone formation and resorption.

PTH signaling Formation Resorption Reformation

Imaging and analytics

Longitudinal imaging and quantitative analysis

I combine two photon microscopy, second harmonic generation imaging, fluorescent cell tracking, tissue clearing, Imaris reconstruction, and MATLAB analysis to follow cellular and extracellular matrix changes through time.

Two photon imaging SHG Imaris MATLAB ImageJ and Fiji
Experimental workflow

Following remodeling through multiple phases

Longitudinal imaging allows the same three dimensional tissue to be evaluated across formation, resorption, and regenerative recovery.

01

Formation

Osteogenic cells generate mineralized bone-like tissue and collagen-rich extracellular matrix.

02

Resorption

Osteoclast-lineage cells remodel the matrix and generate localized resorption regions.

03

Reformation

Osteoblast-lineage cells respond to the remodeled microenvironment and generate new matrix.

Methods

Integrated experimental capabilities

The research platform connects biological experimentation, advanced imaging, molecular analysis, and computational quantification.

Tissue engineering

Functional bone models, osteospheroid systems, long-term culture, multicellular labeling, and remodeling assays.

Advanced imaging

Two photon microscopy, SHG imaging, fluorescence tracking, tissue clearing, micro-CT, and three dimensional reconstruction.

Quantitative analysis

Imaris segmentation, MATLAB pipelines, ImageJ and Fiji analysis, repeated-measures statistics, and scientific visualization.
