Professional profile

Building an independent path in regenerative bone research

My long-term goal is to become an independent researcher developing controllable tissue-engineering platforms for regenerative bone medicine.

My current work focuses on three-dimensional functional bone models, PTH-regulated remodeling, osteoblast and osteoclast coupling, second harmonic generation imaging, tissue clearing, and quantitative three-dimensional analysis.

I aim to connect mechanistic bone biology with biomaterials, advanced imaging, reproducible computation, and therapeutic tissue regeneration.
