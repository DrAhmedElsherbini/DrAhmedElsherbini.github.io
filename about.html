Professional profile

Building an independent path in regenerative bone research

My long term goal is to become an independent researcher developing controllable tissue engineering platforms for regenerative bone medicine.

My current work focuses on three dimensional functional bone models, PTH regulated remodeling, osteoblast and osteoclast coupling, second harmonic generation imaging, tissue clearing, and quantitative three dimensional analysis. I am particularly interested in platforms that connect mechanistic studies with biomaterial design and therapeutic regeneration.
