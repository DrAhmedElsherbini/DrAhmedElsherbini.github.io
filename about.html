Professional profile

Building an independent path in regenerative bone research

My long-term goal is to become an independent researcher developing controllable tissue engineering platforms for regenerative bone medicine.

My current work focuses on three-dimensional functional bone models, PTH-regulated remodeling, osteoblast and osteoclast coupling, second harmonic generation imaging, tissue clearing, and quantitative three-dimensional analysis.

I am particularly interested in platforms that connect mechanistic biological studies with biomaterial design, regenerative therapies, and translational bone research.

Technical profile

Methods and capabilities

My technical experience spans experimental bone biology, three-dimensional imaging, molecular analysis, quantitative workflows, and scientific communication.

Bone biology and tissue engineering

Three-dimensional functional bone models, osteospheroid models, remodeling assays, PTH experiments, and osteoblast, osteoclast, and osteocyte-related workflows.

Imaging and visualization

Two-photon microscopy, SHG imaging, light microscopy, electron microscopy, micro-CT, Imaris, NIS-Elements, ImageJ, and Fiji.

Molecular and wet laboratory

qPCR, single-cell PCR, multicellular labeling, staining, DNA recombination, transfection, transduction, and drug administration studies.

Quantitative analysis

MATLAB workflows for image-derived measurements, repeated measures analysis, multiple comparisons, data visualization, and working knowledge of Python and R.

Tissue clearing

CUBIC and LUCID protocols for internal cellular organization, matrix visualization, and three-dimensional reconstruction.

Research communication

Scientific writing, manuscript preparation, peer-review responses, visual communication, presentations, and reproducible code documentation.
